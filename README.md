# GreatLLM

Some free LLM, vision models, image... All free.

## Free providers

| Provider | Free access | Best for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [NVIDIA NIM](https://build.nvidia.com/) | Free endpoints available | Open models, high-throughput inference | NVIDIA's catalog currently exposes free endpoints for selected models. Community testing reports good availability, but limits and model availability can change. |
| [Cloudflare Workers AI](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/) | 10,000 Neurons/day on Workers Free | Serverless AI, edge inference | Many models remain available on the Free plan. Community reports mixed latency/reliability, so it is better suited to experimentation than latency-sensitive workloads. |
| [Z.AI](https://z.ai/) | GLM-4.7-Flash and other Flash models are free | Coding, reasoning, Chinese/English | GLM-4.7-Flash is officially listed as completely free with a 200K context window. |
